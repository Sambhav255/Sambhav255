I built all of it and then ran it.

## 🔬 Featured projects

### **Vigil** | AI-powered watchlist platform
Real time portfolio monitoring with AI driven alerts on price movement, news sentiment, and market opportunities. React, TypeScript, Firebase.

### **Ruta'al** | Financial inclusion for informal economies
USSD based fintech for unbanked informal workers in Mexico and Nepal. Took it from customer discovery to working prototype and won $6,000 across three business competitions.

### **CardSense** | Credit card optimizer
Reads your transaction patterns and tells you which card you should actually be using, plus what the wrong one is costing you annually across 15+ card products. React, Gemini API, Vercel.

### **VibeTicker** | Real time market sentiment dashboard
Compares AI analyzed financial news against live equity, ETF, and crypto price feeds to surface divergences between what the news says and what the market is doing. TypeScript, Gemini API.

### **CineMatch** | Talk-to-search movie recommendations
Natural language and voice search over TMDB, built with FastAPI and Gemini, developed with Claude Code and shipped on Vercel.

### **Cortex AI Customer Intelligence** | Data engineering
End to end feedback pipeline running 7 Snowflake Cortex AI functions entirely in SQL, no external ML tooling. dbt transformation layer across staging, intermediate, and marts models with 22 automated schema tests.

### **Interactive Portfolio** | [sambhav.app](https://www.sambhav.app/)
Scroll driven portfolio built with React, Three.js, and GSAP.
